🌋The story, plainly

The ShellPit is the public memory of Terrible Turtle Camp — our Burning Man 2026 build at Phoenix Gulch. It's full of real people: building, laughing, cursing at rebar, talking story at dusk. That's the whole point.

And that's exactly why this page exists. Consent here is a living door — it opens both ways. You can be in the archive today and gone from it tomorrow. You can be assumed-in today and explicitly-yes tomorrow. Nothing on this site — no clip, no photo, no transcript, no good story — is worth more than a single person's comfort.

📋Where things stand — radical honesty edition

On 2026-07-29, camp command (Shaka) assumed consent for all footage currently held — 35 videos and 15 photos — so the archive could open to the world.

In the ledger, every item's stamp flipped from pending to assumed. That stamp is public and machine-checkable. We will never quietly pretend "assumed" was "yes."

"Assumed" is not "explicit." Say it plain: it means the people in this footage haven't each been asked yet. It means we believe our campmates trust us — and we're staking our name on that trust, in public, with the door open behind us.

🟡 assumed Camp command assumed consent (2026-07-29). Overruled instantly by the person it's about. Most items are here.
🟢 explicit The person said yes, by name, on the record. We're collecting these person-by-person. The ledger celebrates every upgrade.
⚫ revoked Someone said no — or just "remove." The media is pulled, the ledger remembers the decision, and the site forgets the person.

Explicit yeses are being collected campmate by campmate. Anyone can overrule the assumption at any moment — no permission from us needed, because it was never ours to give.

⚙️What happens when you email "remove"

  1. The media comes down — video, photo, audio, and its transcript pulled from the site.
  2. The ledger is stamped — the item's consent flips to revoked, with a timestamp. The decision is remembered so it's never quietly re-published.
  3. The search index is purged — your name and words stop surfacing.
  4. Frames and audio are purged — extracted stills, thumbnails, and audio rips go too. Not just the front door — the whole house.
  5. You get a confirmation email back — a human tells you it's done.

Target: within 24 hours of your email, usually much faster.
